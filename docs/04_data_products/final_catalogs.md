# Final Catalogs & Photometric Products

FORECAST outputs dust- and IGM-corrected particle catalogs as well as integrated galaxy catalogs.

---

## 1. `flux.dc.<snap>_<plane>.txt` (Dust-Corrected Particle Catalog)
- **Columns 1–8**: Particle identifiers and properties (`subhalo_id`, `x`, `y`, `z`, `mass`, `init_mass`, `metallicity`, `age`).
- **Columns 9–10**: Measured galaxy-averaged gas properties:
  - Col 9: $\langle Z_{\text{gas}} \rangle$ (Gas metallicity in $Z_\odot$)
  - Col 10: $N_{\text{HI}}$ (Neutral hydrogen column density in $\text{kpc}^{-2}$)
- **Columns 11–12**: Particle counts ($N_{p,\text{sh}}$ in lightcone, $N_{\text{sim}}$ in simulation).
- **Columns 13 to $12 + N_{\text{filters}}$**: Dust-attenuated stellar fluxes $f_{\text{dc}, 1}, \dots, f_{\text{dc}, N}$ in $\mu\text{Jy}$.

---

## 2. `flux.igm.<snap>_<plane>.txt` (Final Corrected Particle Catalog)
- **Columns 1–12**: Identical metadata to `flux.dc`.
- **Columns 13 to $12 + N_{\text{filters}}$**: Final observed stellar fluxes $f_{\text{igm}, 1}, \dots, f_{\text{igm}, N}$ in $\mu\text{Jy}$ including k-correction, dust attenuation, and IGM absorption.

---

## 3. Merged Galaxy Catalog (`galaxy_catalog.fits` / `.txt`)
Generated by `postprocessing/galaxy_cat.py`:
- `SUBHALO_ID`: Simulation subhalo index
- `RA`, `DEC`: Projected celestial coordinates (deg)
- `Z_COSMO`: Redshift
- `M_STAR`: Total integrated stellar mass ($M_\odot$)
- `SFR`: Star formation rate ($M_\odot / \text{yr}$)
- `METALLICITY_MASS_WEIGHTED`: Average stellar metallicity
- `FLUX_<FILTER>`: Integrated galaxy flux ($\mu\text{Jy}$)
- `MAG_AB_<FILTER>`: Apparent AB magnitude
